# 🇰🇷 Korea Winter Trip · 1–15 Jan 2027

An interactive, self-contained trip planner for a two-week winter rail trip around South Korea, for two travellers.

**👉 Open the planner:** [https://joelsmithucl.github.io/seoul-trip-2027/](https://joelsmithucl.github.io/seoul-trip-2027/)

(Or open [`seoul-trip-planner.html`](seoul-trip-planner.html) directly in any browser — it's one file, no build step, works offline.)

## The trip at a glance

- **Arrive:** Fri 1 Jan 2027, 16:25 (Incheon ICN)
- **Depart:** Fri 15 Jan 2027, 13:20
- **Travellers:** 2 · **Budget:** mid-range · **Rail:** KORAIL 5-Day Flexible Pass
- **Route:** Seoul (friends, then hotel) → single southern loop by rail → back to Seoul to fly home

## What's in the planner

- 📅 **Clickable calendar** — tap any day to jump to its details
- 🗓️ **Day-by-day itinerary** (palaces, DMZ, temples, museums, markets, nightlife)
- 🍜 **Food & nightlife by city** with map links
- 💡 **Practical tips** — getting-around apps, staying connected (eSIM), money, etiquette
- 🧳 **Winter packing list** (tickable)
- 🎟️ **Book-ahead vs. walk-in** guide + booking checklist

## Notes

- Draft plan — always re-confirm DMZ/JSA, train times and opening hours closer to departure.
- Map links open in Naver Map (the most reliable maps in Korea).
