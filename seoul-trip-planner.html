🧭 How this trip flows

Seoul first (3 nights with friends, then 2 nights at your own hotel), then a single southern loop by rail — Jeonju → Busan → Gyeongju — with no backtracking, so you land back in Seoul for 3 full days before flying home.

🚄 How the KORAIL Pass actually works: there is no "10-day" pass. You choose a pass of 2, 3, 4, or 5 flexible travel days, and you use those days on any dates you like within a 10-day window that begins on your chosen start date (travel days need not be consecutive). We're using a 5-Day Flexible Saver Pass (for 2 travelling together): the southern loop needs just 4 travel days — Jan 6, 7, 10, 12 (Seoul→Jeonju→Busan→Gyeongju→Seoul), leaving 1 spare, all inside the 10-day window. Reserve seats early — since Oct 2026, KTX seats open two months before departure.
⏱️ Departure-day math: 13:20 flight → be at ICN by ~11:00 → AREX is ~43 min, so leave central Seoul by ~09:30. Jan 14 is your last real day; Jan 15 is airport-only.

💡 Getting around: apps & useful tips

🗺️ Maps & navigationinstall before you fly
  • Naver Map — your primary. Best English support of the Korean apps, dense coverage of restaurants & venues, and excellent subway/bus directions with live timing. Default to this for everything. The map links on this page all open in Naver.
  • Kakao Map — keep as backup. A good cross-check when Naver gives an odd result; some locals rate its walking directions & certain POIs slightly higher.
  • ⚠️ Google Maps is limited in Korea — no car navigation and patchy transit/walking directions (due to local map-data export rules). Fine for previewing a location, not for navigating on the ground.
  • One-liner: Navigate with Naver, hail taxis & message with Kakao.
  • Tip: try searching a spot now (e.g. 명동교자 / Myeongdong Kyoja) so the interface is familiar before you land.
📱 Other essentialscomms · taxis · pay · transit
  • KakaoTalk — Korea's dominant messaging app. A lot of bookings, guesthouses & tour operators communicate through it; handy for your friends too.
  • Kakao T — the standard taxi-hailing app, pairs with your Kakao account.
  • T-money card — tap-to-ride for subway/bus (and many convenience-store purchases); top up at any CU/GS25 or station machine.
  • eSIM / pocket wifi — set up data on arrival at ICN (or buy an eSIM before you fly) so the maps & apps work from the moment you land.
  • Winter note: January in Korea is cold & dry (often below freezing) — phone batteries drain fast in the cold, so carry a power bank for a day of map use.
