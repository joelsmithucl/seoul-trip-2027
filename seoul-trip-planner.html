A two-week rail trip for two · Seoul · Busan · Gyeongju · Jeonju
✈️ Arrive Fri 1 Jan 2027, 16:25 (ICN) 🛫 Depart Fri 15 Jan 2027, 13:20 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 2 travellers 💶 Mid-range 🚄 KORAIL 5-Day Flexible Pass

🧭 How this trip flows

North to south by rail, then back to Seoul to fly home. You stay with friends in Seoul for the first 3 nights, loop down through the historic south, and return to Seoul for the final stretch.

SEG 1Seoul (friends) · 3 nts
SEG 2Busan · 3 nts
SEG 3Gyeongju · 2 nts
SEG 4Temple stay · 1 nt
SEG 5Jeonju · 2 nts
SEG 6Seoul · 2 nts
🚄 KORAIL Pass reality check: there is no "10-day" pass — passes come in 2/3/4/5 days and the multi-day ones are flexible within a 10-day activation window (that's where "10 days" comes from). The 5-Day Flexible Saver Pass (for 2 travelling together) covers all four intercity hops with a spare day. Reserve seats early — since Oct 2026, KTX seats open two months before departure.
⏱️ Departure-day math: 13:20 flight → be at ICN by ~11:00 → AREX is ~43 min, so leave central Seoul by ~09:30. Jan 14 is your last real day; Jan 15 is airport-only.
